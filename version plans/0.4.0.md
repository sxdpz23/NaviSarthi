# NaviSarthi 0.4.0 — Navigation and music pilot

Status: Planned

## Focus

Add readiness/setup guidance, saved console connection, joystick zoom with bounded levels and debounce, round-safe upper viewport layout, next-turn distance/ETA and helmet TTS audio focus. Add console music information and controls through the phone app, brought forward from 0.7.0. Keep playback on the phone/headset and preserve its selected audio output. Qualify Guerrilla/Himalayan first and Bear separately.

## Music scope and implementation decisions

- Control the phone's active music session from the console: play/pause, next track and previous track. Show available track title and artist using the existing console now-playing protocol. Spotify and YouTube Music are the initial qualification targets; supported actions depend on the active player.
- Reuse and audit the inherited MediaInfoProvider, console metadata forwarding and joystick event handling. Select the intended active music session, reflect its playback state, and check supported transport actions before dispatch. Do not report success when no eligible session exists or an action is unavailable.
- Provide optional music enablement and notification-access setup in the app, with clear status for missing access, no active player and unsupported actions. Revoking access or disabling music must clear stale metadata and stop media control/observation without interrupting navigation. Do not request unrelated call permissions for music.
- Confirm console button codes on hardware before finalizing play/pause and track mappings. Preserve navigation zoom, console Home behavior and existing call handling; document mode/priority rules and debounce repeated events so one press performs one action. Do not invent an unverified play/pause code.
- Preserve the phone/headset audio route. Navigation speech uses audio focus/ducking and music recovers afterward; NaviSarthi does not stream music to the console or require a music-provider account/API integration.

## Acceptance

Parked-bike setup; joystick mapping and zoom; no clipping of critical guidance; lock/unlock and app switching; Bluetooth directions over music; Wi-Fi plus cellular; reconnect. Release pilot only after 0.2 reliability and 0.3 routing checks pass.

For music, verify both Spotify and YouTube Music on each qualified console/firmware:

- Now-playing title/artist and playback state follow player changes, pause/resume and track changes without stale information.
- Console play/pause, next and previous each trigger once per press; unavailable actions and absent sessions fail gracefully. Check competing media sessions and player switching.
- Navigation zoom and guidance remain responsive during music control; call events and Home retain their intended behavior without accidental playback commands.
- Playback remains on the selected phone/headset output; TTS ducks/interrupts as intended and playback recovers after guidance.
- Controls and metadata survive phone lock, app switching and console reconnect. Granting, denying or revoking notification access and toggling music off/on recover without crashes or unsolicited playback.
