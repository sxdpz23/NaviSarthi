# NaviSarthi 0.2.0 - Navigation reliability

Status: Development build 0.2.0-dev01; hardware qualification pending.

## Focus

Diagnose GPS, background execution and encoder latency before changing console frame rates. Retain 526 x 300, 4 fps active / 2 fps idle until parked-bike measurements justify another profile. No console modifications.

## Implemented in dev01

- Monotonic capture-time freshness checks on live and cached locations: reject missing/future timestamps, fixes older than 4 seconds, and accuracy worse than 50 metres. Old or inaccurate positions cannot drive turn announcements, route progress or rerouting.
- Initial route requests retry after a fresh origin becomes available; destination changes cancel obsolete initial requests.
- GPS health and bounded prediction use fix capture age rather than callback receipt time. Stale location remains visibly lost instead of appearing current.
- Stream pacing uses elapsed realtime. RTP timestamps follow actual encoder presentation timing, including stalls, rather than assigning every delayed output the current loop timestamp.
- Codec output honours buffer offset/size and always releases acquired output buffers after send errors. Surface failures reach the recovery path. At most three submitted frames may remain pending before recovery, with a two-second output watchdog; no dependent H.264 frames are arbitrarily dropped.
- Foreground wake/Wi-Fi lock acquisition is idempotent. Stop uses stopService. A process-dead foreground service does not restart alone while falsely implying its in-memory navigation pipeline survived.
- Local ride logs include fix age, render duration, encode/send duration, pending frame count, cadence and encoder recoveries. Keep logs local for private testing.

## Remaining work and release gates

- Hardware evidence: 60-minute screen-locked ride, app switching, plus a two-hour soak. Target open-sky displayed location age within 2 seconds, no accumulating backlog, no multi-minute freezes. Record actual display lag, decoder recovery, battery/temperature and console firmware per model.
- Simulate degraded GPS, packet loss and reconnect on device; confirm loss indication and recovery without false turns/reroutes. Current automated checks cover timestamp freshness and RTP packetization, not physical decoder behaviour.
- Ride ownership remains in the Activity-scoped ViewModel, with the foreground service holding background locks. Locking and app switching do not normally clear that ViewModel. Full service-owned runtime/process recovery remains to be designed and tested before claiming survival of Activity destruction. Force-stop/swiping away is outside the requirement.
- Console sockets already bind individually to console Wi-Fi; no process-wide network binding was introduced. Validate cellular routing and tile requests while connected on each phone.
- Existing tile provider and routing service are inherited; provider selection and multi-stop implementation belong to 0.3.0. Navigation quality and motorcycle suitability are not yet certified.

### dev02

The combined ride-feedback build sends native turn glyphs using the TripperDash++ K1G catalog and keeps the streamed cue. It trims the completed line and matches the cursor to the route when position and heading agree. It latches arrival within five metres of the requested coordinates. Left zooms out and right zooms in. The zoom legend is in the map viewport.

Home opens Google Maps. Route offers Car (OSRM) and Bike (Valhalla motorcycle) and carries the chosen profile through rerouting. Dash offers Light, Dark, Black, TDV and POV. Black recolors raster tiles and can retain faint labels. POV tilts the map surface. The cursor is a larger chevron.

Bike checks remain pending for native glyphs, joystick direction, high-zoom tracking, round-screen placement, arrival and route provider behaviour.

### dev04 ride feedback

- Arrival uses a fresh fix within 15 metres, clears the route highlight, and shows elapsed navigation time and a MINI map. Leaving the radius dismisses MINI without resuming the completed route.
- Home navigation returns to the existing Home entry when possible. Tripper route cards hide the secondary glyph and hide the primary glyph while idle.
- Black uses CARTO label-free tiles with map attribution. Its optional gold accent applies to the route and location circle.
- POV uses a gentler camera tilt centred on the rider. Route corners use bounded curves and the rider marker uses a white arrow on a coloured circle.
- Saved destinations have a visible Delete action with confirmation.
- Scheduled Drive JSON backups remain pending Google OAuth configuration and a design for unattended access. Verify the dash changes on the bike before qualification.
