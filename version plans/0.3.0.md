# NaviSarthi 0.3.0 — Multi-stop routing

Status: Planned

## Focus

Evaluate India motorcycle routing/geocoding providers against coverage, share-link resolution, terms and pay-as-you-go budget. Keep provider adapters replaceable. Import Google Maps shared places with confirmation on ambiguous locations. Support ordered stops, insertion, removal, reordering and explicit arrival/skip. Reroute remaining stops with debounce and accuracy gating; retain current route on temporary request failure.

## Acceptance

Verify short/full Maps links, coordinates, malformed shares and unresolved names. Test all three share actions during navigation, multi-stop progression, off-route recovery, network failure and out-of-order responses. Validate motorcycle restrictions on representative Indian roads before provider selection.

## Evidence and decisions

Not yet implemented/verified for this milestone. Record implementation results here; resolve provider/hardware decisions before the affected work.

## dev01 multi-stop pilot

- Plan up to eight ordered stops from Google Maps shares and saved destinations. Each additional place offers Add next, Append or Replace. Stops can be moved up or removed before starting.
- Preview each leg with the selected Car or Bike profile and show aggregate distance and duration. The dash guides one leg at a time, advances at 15 metres, and offers Skip this stop. Rerouting affects the active leg. The final stop uses the existing arrival view and whole-journey elapsed time.
- Sharing during navigation starts from the unvisited stop list. Tap Start navigation to apply the revised plan. The itinerary is session-local. Named route saving is a later milestone.
- Parked-bike acceptance: preview three stops in both profiles, reorder/remove, start, reach the first stop, miss a turn on the next leg, skip an intermediate stop, and verify final arrival. Confirm an added place during navigation does not reintroduce a completed stop.

## dev02 saved itineraries

Route can save a named copy of the ordered stop list and Car/Bike mode. Saved itineraries survive an app or phone restart and can be opened or deleted from Route. Opening recalculates the route from the current origin. Saved copies are local to the phone and do not store active ride progress.

## dev03 offline route downloads

Saved itineraries can download the ordered route legs and turn instructions from the current GPS origin. Downloads are stored in app files and can be opened after restart without contacting a routing service. Downloaded guidance follows the fixed route, including after advancing or skipping stops. Automatic recalculation and dedicated offline basemap downloads are separate features. Check opening after restart with mobile data disabled and then verify stop transitions on the bike.
