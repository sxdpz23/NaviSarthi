Requested release: 0.6.2 (versionCode 33). Build authorized after verification on 2026-11-17.

## Scope

- One Nav-owned route/guidance/map session, automatically displayed on the selected Dash/Pod.
- Explicit temporary handset viewing/editing; suppress duplicate console previews and remove the separate Console route page/handoff action.
- Car/Motorbike route choices, genuine motorcycle costing online/offline, existing Nav lists renamed Saved Destinations.
- Consistent darker glass across taskbar, map controls, place/route panels and mobile guidance; matching portrait margins and docked bottom panels.
- Balanced compact taskbar; one separated stack for mobile navigation view controls; remove navigation pause.
- Correct traffic/road order and stronger highlighted route contrast, including Nav traffic hues on Dash.
